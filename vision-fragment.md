
## 1. Product Vision

### Problem Statement
Gamers with extensive Steam libraries struggle to find relevant games to play, both new titles and existing "backlog" entries, due to overwhelming, marketing-driven store interfaces and a lack of personalized, timely insights about game updates.

### Target Audience
**The "Steam Power User":** Gamers who have accumulated a significant library of games (backlog) and lack the time or desire to sift through generic, ad-heavy store pages to find their next experience.

### Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
BacklogCompanion cuts through the noise of the Steam Store by using AI to analyze your personal playing habits and library, delivering highly relevant recommendations for both **new discoveries** and **backlog revival** (e.g., major updates to owned games), all in a clean, focused mobile interface.

### Key Differentiators
1.  **AI-Driven Relevance:** Recommendations based on verified playtime and preferences, not publisher marketing budgets.
2.  **Backlog Revival:** Proactively surfacing reasons to return to owned games (e.g., massive content updates), transforming a "pile of shame" into a library of opportunity.
3.  **Mobile-First Focus:** A dedicated, streamlined companion app experience separate from the desktop-heavy store environment.

### Vision Statement
To be the essential mobile companion for Steam gamers, turning the overwhelming choice of modern gaming into a curated, personalized journey of discovery and rediscovery.
