
## 3. Success Metrics

### North Star Metric
**"Recommendation-to-Playtime Ratio":** The percentage of total monthly playtime spent in games that were *specifically recommended* by BacklogCompanion vs. games found elsewhere.
*   *Why:* It proves the app isn't just suggesting games users *might* like, but successfully driving the decision to play and sustaining engagement.

### User Success Metrics
*   **Recommendation Conversion Rate:** % of recommended games that are installed and launched within 7 days.
*   **Quality of Play:** Average playtime per recommended game > 5 hours (filtering out "bounce" installs where a user plays for 15 mins and quits).
*   **Satisfaction Score:** % of "Yes" responses to "Did you enjoy game X?" prompts after significant playtime milestones.

### Business/Product Health Metrics
*   **Retention (The "Habit"):** % of users who return to the app before launching a game on their PC (weekly active users who launch at least one recommended game).
*   **Backlog Activation:** Total number of "dormant" games (owned > 1 year, 0 playtime) revived per month across the user base.
*   **Prediction Accuracy:** Improvement in positive feedback loops ("Do you want more games like X?") over time.
