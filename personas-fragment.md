
## 2. Target Audience & Personas

### Primary Persona: "The Saturated Strategist" (Alex)
*   **Profile:** An experienced PC gamer with a 500+ game Steam library (accumulated over years of bundles and sales). Works full-time, has limited gaming hours (e.g., 5-10 hours/week).
*   **Behavior:** 
    *   spends 80% of their time in 20% of their library (the "safe harbors" like *Castlevania* or a favorite *Extraction Shooter*).
    *   Buys ~20 games a year, mostly during major seasonal sales or high-profile releases in specific favorite genres.
    *   Many owned games are unplayed ("pile of shame") or were free giveaways.
*   **Pain Point:** Boredom with the "same old" experience but fear of wasting limited time on a bad new game. Wants "new impressions" but defaults to familiarity because discovery is too much work.
*   **Needs:** reliable recommendation that bridges the gap between their "safe harbor" tastes and a fresh, exciting experience. "Show me something I'll love that I didn't know I wanted."

### Secondary Persona: "The Deal Hunter" (Sam)
*   **Profile:** Price-conscious gamer who loves getting value.
*   **Behavior:** 
    *   Invests huge hours into a few games they love.
    *   Rarely changes habits unless the barrier to entry is low (cheap price) and social proof is high (overwhelmingly positive reviews).
*   **Pain Point:** Missing out on great experiences because they aren't "trending" or aren't deeply discounted yet.
*   **Needs:** Validation that a cheap/discounted game is actually worth their specific time investment, not just "popular."

### The "Moment of Struggle"
*   **Scenario A (The Bored Veteran):** It's Friday night. Alex is tired of the usual shooter grind. They scroll through their library, feel overwhelmed by 300 unplayed icons, and almost close Steam—or worse, just launch the same game they're bored of. They need a **"Guess my choice I may not be aware of myself"** intervention.
*   **Scenario B (The Time-Crunched dad/professional):** Has 30 minutes. Wants a specific *vibe* (e.g., "something repetitive but interacting like Euro Truck" vs. "something epic like Space Marine 2"). Needs an instant filter for "Quality Short Session" or "Epic Campaign" based on current mood.
